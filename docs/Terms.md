## 2. What Knokio is

Knokio is a privacy-first inbound filtering service. It provides:
- A public entry point ("door") that replaces sharing personal contact details
- Structured request submission for people trying to reach you
- Filtering, caps, and controls for managing inbound volume
- Controlled contact reveal only when you choose

Knokio is **not** a messaging platform, social network, marketplace, or directory.

## 5. Doors and requests

### 5.1 Door owners (Keepers)
- You control your door's configuration, categories, caps, and reveal settings
- You are responsible for the categories and fields you configure
- Accepting a request reveals your configured contact information to the knocker
- You may decline requests or let them expire without explanation
- You may block senders at your discretion

### 5.2 Request submitters (Knockers)
- By submitting a request, you consent to your provided information being shared with the door owner
- Requests are one-shot submissions; there is no threading or follow-up mechanism
- There is no guarantee that a door owner will respond to your request
- Repeated or abusive submissions may result in rate limiting or blocking

### 5.3 No guarantee of delivery
We make reasonable efforts to deliver requests and notifications, but do not guarantee delivery. Email deliverability depends on third-party infrastructure and recipient configuration.
